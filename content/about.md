I am a statistician specialized in experimental design: deciding which experiments to run.
Often, researchers perform their experiments first and only talk to a statistician afterwards.
Much time and effort can be saved by planning the experiment together beforehand,
with a design made for the specific measurement setup, goals, and budget.

My research focuses on dynamic systems, where the model is a set of differential equations and the design is a control profile over time,
and on adaptive Bayesian experimental design, where the next experiment is chosen based on the measurements gathered so far.
More recently, I have started looking at experimental design for improving AI models, such as world models.
New papers and talks are announced on the [blog]({{< ref "posts" >}}).

Through my consulting company, Strouwen Statistics, I work with researchers in academia and industry on:
- planning experiments: screening, response surface, mixture, and optimal designs, blocked and split-plot experiments, and Bayesian optimization,
- model-based experimental design for dynamic systems, including adaptive designs,
- statistical modelling: Bayesian and mixed effects models, and [small sample inference](https://github.com/ArnoStrouwen/MixedModelsSmallSample.jl),
- learning missing physics in differential equation models, and [designing experiments for it](https://docs.sciml.ai/Overview/stable/showcase/optimal_data_gathering_for_missing_physics/),
- scientific software in Julia,
- courses and training on experimental design.

At JuliaHub and PumasAI, I have worked on software for drug development:
quantitative systems pharmacology (QSP), noncompartmental analysis (NCA), bioequivalence, in vitro–in vivo correlation (IVIVC),
and physiologically based pharmacokinetic and biopharmaceutics models (PBPK and PBBM).
Before that, at Johnson & Johnson, I designed accelerated stability studies to predict the shelf life of vaccines and other drug products,
optimized the manufacturing conditions of chemical reactions with high-throughput experiments,
and built Bayesian mixed effects models for the flowability of powders.
Before joining industry, I did my PhD as a Research Foundation Flanders (FWO) fellow at KU Leuven,
designing dynamic experiments to find out how to keep fruit and vegetables fresh longer.

I also teach the [Experimental Design](https://lstat.kuleuven.be/training/coursedescriptions/experimental_designnew) course in the Master of Statistics and Data Science at KU Leuven.
In open source, I am a significant contributor to the [documentation](https://docs.sciml.ai/) of the Scientific Machine Learning (SciML) ecosystem,
and to [SciMLSensitivity](https://docs.sciml.ai/SciMLSensitivity/stable/), which computes the derivatives of differential equation solutions with respect to their parameters.

If you are planning experiments, or want a second opinion on a design, you can reach me at [contact@arnostrouwen.com](mailto:contact@arnostrouwen.com).
